Hi, my name is Daphne.

I'm a recent graduate of Columbia University and Trinity College Dublin, where I completed a Dual BA in Neuroscience & Behaviour and Biomedical Sciences. My interests sit at the intersection of neuroscience, medicine, law, and bioethics — particularly the ethical and legal questions that arise at the boundaries of medicine and emerging neuroscience.

I have worked with David N. Hoffman, JD, on research examining Medical Aid in Dying (MAiD) and the legal and ethical frameworks that govern it across the United States. I currently work with Brendan Parent, JD, and colleagues at NYU Langone on medical ethics research examining brain resilience and the medical and legal determination of death — including its implications for resuscitation, organ donation, and transplantation protocols.
